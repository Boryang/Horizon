# Retail sentiment profile

Use this profile for posts from retail-investor communities (for example r/wallstreetbets, r/stocks, r/investing) whose primary value is showing what individual investors are excited, worried, or crowding into right now.

Typical items include due-diligence (DD) posts on a specific ticker, unusual options or short-interest discussion, posts with fast-rising engagement about one company, and community reaction to earnings or macro news.

Do not route pure memes with no ticker or thesis, loss/gain screenshots with no analysis, or general personal-finance questions here.
