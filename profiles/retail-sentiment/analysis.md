# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a retail-investor community post is an early, specific, and evidence-backed signal that a stock or theme is drawing unusual retail attention — the kind of signal that preceded episodes such as GameStop in 2021.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Strong signal.** A detailed thesis on a named ticker with verifiable figures (short interest, float, cash, filings, options open interest) AND unusually high engagement or comments showing many participants acting on it.
- **7-8: Notable.** A named ticker or theme with a concrete, checkable thesis and clearly above-normal engagement, or a sharp shift in community sentiment on a widely held stock.
- **5-6: Context.** Useful snapshot of what the community is discussing, but the thesis is thin or engagement is ordinary.
- **3-4: Low value.** Generic bullish or bearish opinions, repeated tickers with nothing new, questions.
- **0-2: Noise.** Memes, gain/loss screenshots, pump language without evidence, obvious promotion.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward specificity (ticker, numbers, sources) and evidence that the post is moving people, such as score and comment count relative to the subreddit. Penalize claims that cannot be checked. Popularity alone is not importance; a post needs both a concrete thesis and unusual attention to score above 7. Name the ticker(s) in the tags.

Use three to five specific topic tags, including the ticker symbol when present.
